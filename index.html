What the dashboard does — and doesn't — show

Voting records come from congressional sources. Roll calls show named positions; voice votes and some other legislative actions do not identify each member's vote.

Some votes are on amendments, procedures, nominations, or motions—not final passage of a bill. Always read the linked official record before forming a conclusion.

Statements are not automatically attributed to votes. Approval polling is intentionally omitted until a reputable, date-stamped source can be verified.

Your ratings and topic choices are stored in this browser only. No account or cloud synchronization is included.
